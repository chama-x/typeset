Theoretical Foundation

Five Non-Lossy Physical Invariants

Typography treated as an optical physics derivation rather than arbitrary styling.

Law 1

Surface Entropy Allocation (85 / 15 Split)

A high-craft interface strictly allocates ≥ 85% area to functional reading and ≤ 15% to sculptural signature memory.

Area(Core ∪ Substrate) ≥ 0.85 • Area(Signature) ≤ 0.15
Law 2

Logarithmic Optical Tracking τ(s)

Letter spacing decreases logarithmically as glyph size increases, preventing loose display titles and cramped micro labels.

τ(s) = clamp(-0.045em, -0.022 • ln(s / 16)em, +0.025em)
Law 3

Hyperbolic Leading & Measure λ(s, M)

Line height scales inversely with font size, modulated by line measure in character widths (constrained to 45ch–70ch for prose).

λ(s, M) = 1.02 + 8.5/s + 0.003 • max(0, M - 55)
Law 4

Harmonic Fluid Scale S(n, v)

Linear slope-intercept derivation between mobile (360px) and desktop (1440px) viewports with zero sudden media-query breakpoint jumps.

font-size(n) = clamp(S_min rem, b rem + 100m vw, S_max rem)
Law 5

Optical x-Height Normalization & TrueGrade Polarity

Equalizes optical perceived weight when mixing secondary italic serifs into grotesques via font-size-adjust: ex-height from-font, and counters OLED halation in dark mode with GRAD: -25 without triggering layout shift reflows.

GRAD(P) = -25 • P  |  Δw(P) = -35 • P  |  S_2 = S_1 • (x_1 / x_2)
